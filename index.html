How grading works

Each problem is split into a few steps. Write your method in the work area, put the result in the answer box and press Check step.

  • A wrong answer gets a nudge, a question to ask yourself. Nudges get more specific with each try.
  • You get three tries and then one final attempt. If that misses too, the step's answer is shown and you carry on with the next step yourself.
  • Hint for this step opens a hint only when you press it. It doesn't use a try, but it is recorded.
  • Submit question prints how each step went and unlocks the checked answer once every step is closed.

The pen

Press Pen (or the P key) to draw on any page while explaining. On the notes page the ink sticks to the text as you scroll, and a finger scrolls while a stylus or mouse draws. On the lesson and practice pages the ink is kept separately for each problem. Nothing is saved when the tab closes.
