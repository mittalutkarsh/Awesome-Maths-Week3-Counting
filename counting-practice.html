Rules to remember

Rule of product
Same number of options at each step → multiply.
Numbers
No leading zero. Zero is even.
Restricted first
Settle the awkward rule before the rest.
n different things
n! arrangements. 0! = 1.
No repeats
Options shrink by one each step.
Repeated letters
n! ÷ (each repeat count)!, multiplied together.
Identical groups
÷ (number of groups)!
Sets
Built in order? ÷ k! for k items.
Double counting
Counted from both sides? ÷ 2.
